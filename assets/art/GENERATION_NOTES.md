# Generated pixel art

The source art was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool in three coordinated passes:

1. Ancient Greek labyrinth environment atlas using dark limestone, bronze, moss, amber, and cyan accents.
2. Explorer A and Explorer B animation atlas with rust-red and muted teal costume palettes.
3. Spirits, Minotaur, owl, and Athena animation atlas.

The attached maze screenshot supplied by the project owner was used as a composition and atmosphere reference. Its exact layout was not copied.

The generated masters are preserved in `work/generated/`. `work/process_pixel_art.py` normalizes them into exact game sheets, transparent padding, nearest-neighbor scaling, and the folder layout under `assets/art/`.

# The Shifting Labyrinth: implementation rules

1. **One grid is authoritative.** Maze occupancy drives TileMapLayer cells, TileSet collision polygons, light occluders, line of sight and AStar. Never maintain a separate hand-positioned wall picture.
2. **Walls join their neighbours.** Use the N/E/S/W bitmask atlas. Exposed edges receive bevels; south edges receive the masonry face. Do not stretch a frontal wall picture over arbitrary rectangles.
3. **Every sector stays connected.** The solo map contains 18×9 chambers: 54 per sector. Shifts every six seconds open connections before closing redundant ones. Flood-fill before accepting a closure. Unlocked border connections also shift, but at least one connection across each unlocked boundary must remain open.
4. **Never build over an occupant.** Reserve the player, living enemies and dropped items, including their clearance radius. A locked connection keeps its exact open/closed state. Rebuild collision, navigation and neighbour tiles together.
5. **Telegraph changes.** Connection sockets pulse amber for two seconds before a shift, show cyan while held, and emit dust on a change. The minimap reflects the actual grid.
6. **Measure source artwork.** The generated masters are concept sheets, not regular atlases. Store crop bounds in `assets/art/sprite_crop_manifest.json`. Do not run the legacy `work/process_pixel_art.py`; `work/polish_assets.py` is the maintained importer.
7. **Keep feet stable.** Explorer frames are 64×64, anchored at (32,60). Preserve down/left/right/up rows. Advance walking by distance actually travelled, stop it against walls, and keep bow aiming separate from the foot cycle. Spirits use a stable hovering pose; never cycle between different viewing directions as an idle animation.
8. **Keep pixels stable.** Nearest texture filtering, rounded render origins, fixed physics updates, and swept circle collision. Camera transforms must never move physics bodies.
9. **Keep interface lighting separate.** World lighting belongs to WorldCanvas, with lights restricted to canvas layer -1. The HUD is unshaded. Darkness falls off around the player's position, and the lantern follows that position.
10. **Verify in Godot.** Check solo progression, collision before/after shifting, locked and occupied passages, shifts in every sector, shrine respawn and the final reveal. Test AI around obstacles during a shift. Keep changes local.
11. **Use one bow origin.** The rendered bow and projectile launch share `bow_muzzle()`. Arrows travel visibly and sweep each movement segment, resolving only the nearest wall/enemy. Never draw a shot from the player's feet.
12. **Retain AI path progress.** Use an advancing waypoint index, never restart pursuit at a passed start-cell centre. Repath after grid changes. Remember the player's last seen position, search, and return if contact is lost. Telegraph attacks before damage.
13. **Explain routes from current state.** Objective text, gold trail and map must update after shifts and guardian deaths. Explain sealed gates and the shrine requirement. F preserves a passage's current state; it does not unlock gates. H explains this in-game.
14. **Keep the approved wall image unchanged.** Its SHA-256 is `8bae96f460949ce6ab3e79d8c03cd730855302be81666a2327c280ee3c3a4329`. Change topology and lighting without regenerating this artwork unless the user asks.
15. **Release the boss only after every spirit dies.** Seal all three connections into chamber (17,4) in rendering, physics and navigation. Exclude this one chamber from the pre-release connectivity count, while keeping every spirit reachable. Neither F, shifting, arrows nor sound may activate the sealed boss. The last spirit's death opens the chamber atomically and starts the hunt.
16. **Keep the Minotaur hunting and visible on maps.** Once released, it targets the player's live position through AStar regardless of distance or line of sight, retaining normal wall collision and attack telegraphs. Repath after shifts and player respawn. Maps read the boss's actual position every draw: gray horns when sealed, pink while alive and released, no marker after death. Restart reseals the chamber.

The attack uses a directionally posed bow over the anchored body. The generated attack poses face sideways even in front/back rows, so they are deliberately excluded from runtime animation. Hurt uses a brief tint, not a wrongly cropped death pose.
